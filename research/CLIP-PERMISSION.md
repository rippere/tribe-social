**Investigator:** Ben Rippere

## What I'm agreeing to

I give Ben Rippere permission to use the video clips I shoot and edit for this study (three base videos, four hook variants each) in the following ways:

1. **Scoring.** The clips are run through TRIBE v2, a brain-encoding model, and through content-feature models. The resulting numbers are stored with the study data.
2. **Paid delivery.** Each clip runs as a paid ad on Instagram Reels, from an account Ben controls, to a general US audience for at least 3 days. The ads are not edited once they start.
3. **Human-guess round.** Other team members, and interviewees who agree, watch and rank the clips.
4. **Publication.** Results are published only as aggregates such as effect sizes, correlations and verdicts. The write-up may describe what a clip shows, and may include still frames only if I agree to that separately below.

## What I confirm

- I shot the clips, or I have permission from whoever did.
- Anyone recognisable in my clips has agreed to appear in a public ad. I will get their written permission and give it to Ben.
- The audio is my own, royalty-free, or licensed for use in ads. No commercial music without a licence.
- The clips don't promote anything in a restricted ad category (alcohol, drugs, weapons, gambling, dating, financial or health claims, politics).

## What I get, and what I can take back

- There is no payment. The ad spend is covered by the study, not by me.
- I can withdraw my clips at any time before publication (`PREREGISTRATION.md` §15). Withdrawing before freeze just means the base is replaced or dropped. After freeze, a withdrawal is logged as a deviation (§12), and my clips are left out of all published material.
- On request, Ben will delete my clips and their stored features once the study is published.
- The study is non-commercial. TRIBE v2 is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0, and the scores from my clips will not be used in a commercial product.
