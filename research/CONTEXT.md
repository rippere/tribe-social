# Hook test

The pre-registered study of whether predicted cortical response adds anything to a content model for short-form hook rate, and the filmed corpus it runs on.

## Language

### The corpus

**Base**:
One product video, filmed by one shooter, that exists in four Variants. The base is the unit of analysis.
_Avoid_: Video, concept, ad

**Variant**:
One of the four finished files of a Base, identical to its siblings except for its Opening.
_Avoid_: Version, cut, ad

**Opening**:
The first 3 seconds (exactly 90 frames) of a Variant; the only part that differs inside a Base. One of `spoken`, `visual`, `text` or `cold`.
_Avoid_: Intro, hook (an opening is a slot; a hook is what may fill it)

**Hook**:
A device placed in an Opening to stop the scroll (a spoken line, an on-screen line, or a motion moment). A `cold` Opening has no hook.
_Avoid_: Opening

**Body**:
Everything after frame 90 of a Variant, shared frame-for-frame by all four Variants of a Base.
_Avoid_: Main video, content

**Plate**:
A plain 3-second shot of the product with no hook, used as the `cold` Opening and as the footage under the `text` Opening.
_Avoid_: B-roll, filler

**Proof moment**:
The roughly 2-second shot in the Body where the viewer can see the product work.
_Avoid_: Money shot, demo

**Base card**:
The one-page plan for a Base, filled in before shooting: product, persona, problem, proof moment, body format, hook lines.
_Avoid_: Brief, script

**Body format**:
The beat structure a Body follows, taken from a fixed menu (problem → solution, demo, honest review, unboxing, 3 reasons, routine integration).
_Avoid_: Template, style

### Outcomes

**Hook rate**:
3-second video plays divided by impressions, as the platform reports them. The primary outcome.
_Avoid_: Thumb-stop rate, view rate

**Hold rate**:
The share of viewers reaching 15 seconds. Secondary, never decisive.
_Avoid_: Retention, watch time

### Buyers

**Hook-Rate Hunter** (Persona C):
A creative strategist or performance marketer at a DTC brand or its agency who launches 10+ creator-style ads a month and already reads hook rate weekly. The primary audience for the corpus.
_Avoid_: Media buyer, marketer

**Draft Gatekeeper** (Persona B):
An influencer-agency campaign manager or brand creator-program lead who approves 20+ creator drafts a month before they post. The secondary audience for the corpus.
_Avoid_: Agency, brand manager

**Working Creator**:
A UGC creator or micro-influencer paid for brand content; the person who makes what Personas B and C judge.
_Avoid_: Influencer, talent
